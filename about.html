About ASL Tours

We simplify exploration by taking care of every detail, so you can focus on living the Kenyan adventure.

Who We Are

ASL Tours & Travel is a passionate Kenyan tour operator based in Eldoret. We specialize in creating immersive journeys that go beyond the guidebook — offering local encounters, hidden wonders, and authentic cultural connections.

Whether you’re looking for a classic Maasai Mara safari, a family camping adventure, hotel bookings, flight arrangements or visa assistance, our team handles everything end-to-end so you can simply enjoy the experience.

  • Individual & group safaris
  • Hotel reservation & flight booking
  • Visa applications & travel insurance
  • Airport transfers & local guides
